What this card is
drawn from

This card has two sources, and every part of it says which one it came from. The narrative and the destination photography are the Marassi Red Sea brochure by Emaar Misr, as supplied — 30 pages. The units, prices, areas and payment terms are the developer’s price list, as supplied in the client sheet, with its own unit renders and floor plans.

The brochure’s own contents page lists 18 sections. The 30 pages supplied carry sections 01 to 04 and stop there. Sections 05 to 18 — which include The Homes — are not in the file, which is why the sixteen units below come from the price list rather than from the brochure.

Nothing here has been calculated, rounded, completed from memory, or taken from any source other than these two. Where both are silent — maintenance and club fees, finishing specification — this card says so rather than filling the gap.
