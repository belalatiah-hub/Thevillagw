About this mockup

The folder, the pocket, the deboss, the card and the studio are the mockup. Every sheet in frame is a page of the supplied Shams Soma sales presentation, reproduced at its original 16:9 proportion — no crop, no recolour, nothing laid over the artwork. Depth is built around the pages: shadows on the surface they rest on, a hairline where the paper is trimmed, and an occlusion line where the pocket or the folder edge passes in front of one.

The navy, the cream and the aqua of the die-cut lip are sampled from the deck itself rather than chosen, and the lip follows the wave that divides its section slides.

Before this goes out

The deck supplied is marked for internal use. Page 1 carries “This document is for internal use only and subject to changes, updates, refinement, and further details”, and every page footer reads “SHAMS SOMA — SALES PRESENTATION | CONFIDENTIAL”.

Clear it with Marakez, or swap in a client-facing edition, before this kit is used as outward-facing collateral.
