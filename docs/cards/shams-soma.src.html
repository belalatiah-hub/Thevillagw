Shams Soma is a place that celebrates nature and outdoor living, where everyday life becomes lighter, more intentional, and deeply connected to the sea and the landscape.

The deck sets out what the place is meant to be: active without being rushed, vibrant without being overwhelming, peaceful without being isolated.

Master plan

Two valleys, running to the sea

What sets it apart

Shaped by the slope

The Valleys

Sloping the whole way, so the sea stays in view.

Two valleys run down through the community to the water — the north from the gate, the south from the town — carrying social gathering, an outdoor gym, a kids area and an infinity pool along the way.

Elevated Living

Homes arranged across different levels.

Following the natural slope of the land to open up views towards the sea. The elevated setting brings more light, privacy and uninterrupted views while creating a natural flow through the community.

The Active Coastline

Seven hundred metres, programmed end to end.

From the sandy beach at one end to the boardwalk at the other, by way of two jetties, the activity ring and a run of elevated decks along the cliff edge.

Along the water

What the coastline
is made of

  • Sandy beach — two stretches with chaise-longues and umbrellas, and a laid-back beach restaurant to grab a bite and enjoy the views.
  • The jetties — two, extending into the sea for swimming and for launching canoes and kayaks, and relaxed spots to pause along the way.
  • Activity Ring — elevated along the cliff edge; a flexible space to work out, meditate or simply pause, doubling as a lookout over the coast.
  • Elevated decks — a series of lookout spaces above the sea, for quiet moments and casual gatherings.
  • Beach bar, beach restaurant and a multi-purpose court.
  • Boardwalk — running the length of it.

Within Somabay

The destination around it

Everything in this section describes Somabay, the destination Shams Soma sits inside — not Shams Soma itself.

A fully integrated, low-density mixed-use destination on the Red Sea, offering a unique community of more than 7,000 residential homes, with over 3,500 currently under construction and more than 1,700 homes already handed over.

Destination facilities

  • The Somabay Marina — mooring, quayside services, retail and dining, with an extension under construction.
  • Golf — an 18-hole, Par 72 Gary Player championship course, a 9-hole Par 3 course, the Hidden Coves course and the Soma Bay Golf Academy.
  • The Sports Arena — gym, Olympic and semi-Olympic pools, football pitches, grass tennis, squash and padel courts, and the S Tennis Academy.
  • Water sports — diving at Orca Dive Center, kitesurfing at 7BFT Kitehouse and Soma Splash Aqua Park.
  • Beaches & promenade — Wyte, Seba and Zouni beaches, a 420 m jetty and a promenade for walking and cycling.
  • The Cascades Spa & Thalasso — an award-winning spa over 7,500 m² with 65 treatment rooms and an 830 m² hydrotherapy pool.
  • Schools — GISS – GEMS International School, Pre-KG to Grade 12, and KOMPASS International School.
