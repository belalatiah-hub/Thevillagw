Within Makadi Heights

The town around it

Everything in this section describes Makadi Heights, the wider town Ledge Valley sits inside — not Ledge Valley itself.

A serene international coastal town by Orascom Development, spanning 4.75 million square meters and reaching 78 meters above sea level. Makadi Heights is designed as a fully integrated town, seamlessly combining residential units, serviced apartments, commercial areas, and hotels, offering a diversified mix of products that cater to a vibrant, year-round lifestyle.

Strategically located in the heart of Makadi Bay, it gives residents and visitors seamless access to all major destinations along the Red Sea coast.

Town facilities

  • Coastal living — beachfront living flows from swimming and kitesurfing to kayaking and snorkeling, with beach lounges and social spots by the Red Sea.
  • Hospitality & entertainment — a curated mix of boutique and full-scale hotels, complemented by The Haus, a landmark venue with 3,400-guest capacity, hosting concerts, weddings, summits, and cultural events year round.
  • Sports & fitness — a dedicated clubhouse with football, padel and tennis courts alongside a fully equipped gym, complemented by tranquil yoga spaces.
  • Retail & dining — a vibrant mix catering to every mood, from casual cafés to curated restaurants, alongside essential outlets for daily needs.
  • Medical — on-site clinics providing essential care and quick health support for residents and visitors.
