About Siyal

A thoughtfully
planned neighbourhood

Rooted in the ease of beachside living, Siyal is a neighbourhood defined by openness, calm, and rhythm — a living canvas where contemporary architecture meets the flow of coastal life.

Siyal is a place where life moves at its own pace, shaped by light, open views, and the quiet presence of water. Homes are designed not just to be lived in, but to be lived with.

The site

Siyal is intentionally designed with a limited footprint representing approximately 12% of the site, ensuring that the majority is dedicated to open landscapes, water features, and communal outdoor spaces.

Location

In the heart of Makadi Bay

Siyal sits inside Makadi Heights, a serene international coastal town by Orascom Development. Spanning 4.75 million square metres and reaching 78 metres above sea level, it is planned as a fully integrated town combining residential units, serviced apartments, commercial areas and hotels.

Strategically located in the heart of Makadi Bay, it gives residents and visitors seamless access to all major destinations along the Red Sea coast.

Design story

Open outward to light

Sculptural, in desert tones

Characterised by soft desert tones and sculptural forms, the architecture is animated by natural light and shifting shadows through the day. Select homes feature double-height entrances and elevated ceilings.

Where life flows beyond walls

Every home is positioned to enjoy open views — over landscaped greenery, tranquil lagoons, or the water features woven through the neighbourhood, so views become an extension of daily living.

Active by nature

The town's walkable design makes daily movement effortless. Thoughtfully planned pathways and accessible amenities create an environment where walking becomes a natural part of everyday life.

Master plan

A seamless fusion between
space and experience

Every element is arranged to form a connection between architecture and everyday living — the majority of the site given to open landscape, lagoons and communal ground.

Within Makadi Heights

The town around it

Siyal's residents draw on the full amenity of the wider town.

  • Hospitality & entertainment — boutique and full-scale hotels, and The Haus, a landmark multipurpose venue with capacity for over 3,400 guests, hosting concerts, weddings, summits and cultural events year round
  • Coastal living — swimming, kitesurfing, kayaking and snorkelling, framed by beach lounges and social spots
  • Sports & fitness — a clubhouse with football, padel and tennis courts, a fully equipped gym, and yoga spaces distributed across the destination
  • Retail & dining — casual cafés and everyday eateries through to curated restaurants, alongside essential retail outlets
  • Medical — on-site clinics providing essential care and quick access to health support
  • Education — a dedicated preschool nurturing early learning for young families
