REAL ESTATE AD EDIT - EDITING REPORT
Source: IMG_2801.mov (untouched copy kept in output/original/)

SOURCE ANALYSIS
- iPhone HEVC 10-bit, Dolby Vision 8.4 / HLG HDR (BT.2020), 1920x1080 stored with -90 deg rotation = 1080x1920 portrait
- 60 fps, 14.46 s, AAC stereo 48 kHz
- Content: ONE continuous handheld take. A presenter walks toward camera speaking, in front of a
  villa that is an unfinished concrete/plaster shell (open window/door openings, sand ground, rubble piles).
- Audio: continuous speech for the full clip, plus heavy wind rumble under ~150 Hz; integrated loudness -11.7 LUFS.

EDITING DECISIONS
- No shot selection or reordering: the clip is a single take with continuous speech, so cutting would
  break the presenter's sentences. The full take is kept. The hook (presenter already moving and talking
  from frame 1, speech starts at 0.4 s) is kept as-is; there is no slow intro or fade-in.
- 0.5 s video+audio fade-out at the end (after the speech ends).

VIDEO PROCESSING ACTUALLY APPLIED (all versions)
1. HDR(HLG) -> SDR BT.709 tonemapping (zscale + Hable), so colors display correctly on Meta/phones
   (uploading raw HLG often looks washed out or oversaturated).
2. Stabilization: vidstab 2-pass, smoothing 20, small static zoom. Measured residual shake was already
   low (~1 px/frame, iPhone OIS/EIS), so this only removes micro-jitter.
3. Light temporal/spatial denoise (hqdn3d 1.2/3).
4. Color grade: +6% contrast, gentle S-curve, slight cool shift in shadows/mids/highlights to neutralize
   the orange cast, -22% saturation in reds/yellows (over-orange sand), +25% saturation in cyan/blue
   (sky slivers), clean whites on the facade and wardrobe.
5. Subtle sharpening (unsharp 0.45).

AUDIO PROCESSING ACTUALLY APPLIED
- Two-stage 95 Hz high-pass (wind rumble reduced ~25-30 dB below 100 Hz), FFT noise reduction,
  -2 dB at 250 Hz (mud), +2.5 dB at 3.2 kHz (speech clarity), 3:1 compression, loudness normalized to
  -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP (a good level for Meta).

LANDSCAPE / VFX ENHANCEMENT - NOT APPLIED (deliberate)
- The property is visibly under construction. Adding lawns, palms, pools, finished facades or a sky
  replacement would misrepresent the project's current state, and the footage contains no existing
  landscaping to enhance. Per the brief ("do not invent amenities / misleading completion claims"),
  no shot received landscape or VFX compositing. Only global color/sky-blue saturation was applied.
- If approved landscape renders or masterplan visuals exist, they should be added as clearly
  labelled "artist's impression" inserts, not composited into this live footage.
